Here is an overview of my research interests and published papers, subjectively arranged based on phenomena and/or methods.

Semiclassical dynamics and formal tools

I work on some formal aspects of the dynamics of quantum condensed matter systems, developing tools to describe them in semiclassical terms, then amenable to elementary "classical mechanics" or path integral calculations.

Unconventional magneto-oscillation phenomena

I study magneto-oscillation phenomena, where a solid state system's thermodynamical or transport properties oscillate as a function of 1/B or B, that go beyond the standard semiclassical paradigm, either due to electronic interactions or to the particular geometry of the setting.

Boltzmann transport in systems of interacting excitations

I investigate non-equilibrium properties of systems consisting of several kinds of particles interacting with each other. How do these interactions reflect in transport signatures, for instance when bound states are formed, when the condensation or breaking of a symmetry by one family of particles is transferred to the whole system?
